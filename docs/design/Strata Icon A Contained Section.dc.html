Contained ground. The near-black block is the icon's own background, so the mark never depends on the taskbar behind it, and near-white becomes usable as a state colour. The 1px amber rim is what keeps the silhouette from dissolving into a dark taskbar.
Risk. A filled square is the most generic silhouette in the tray. The rim and band rhythm carry it, but at 16px among other filled-square icons it competes on colour alone.
Strength. The only direction where near-white works as the scanning state, and the only one whose contrast is completely independent of the taskbar. Bands sit on whole pixels at 2/1/3 thickness, so nothing greys out at 100% DPI.
