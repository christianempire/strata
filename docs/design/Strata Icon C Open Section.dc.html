Mid-tone chroma, no contained ground. Amber holds 10.1:1 against the dark strip but 1.7:1 against the light one — below the 3:1 minimum for non-text contrast. The bars are 2–3px thick so there are no hairlines, but on a light taskbar this mark is a pale shape rather than a legible one. Compare the two strips before choosing it.
Risk. The one direction that can fail on a light taskbar, and near-white is unavailable as a state colour, so scanning has to be carried by bar count instead. Three floating bars are also the easiest of the three to mistake for a generic list or menu glyph.
Strength. Lightest, least utility-looking, and the closest to the app's own visual signature — the bars are the same ragged profile the Timeline draws. Fewest shapes of the three, and it is the only one that reads as a mark rather than a badge.
