Note — aggregation rule
Roll up until it stops explaining. Walking down from the volume root, a directory collapses into one row when a single child accounts for 90% or more of its change. Descent stops when no child dominates — that directory is the honest answer to "where", and its children become the expansion.
The remainder gets a row, not a rounding. Whatever a directory's change is that its named children do not account for appears as elsewhere in …, drawn hatched. Without it the list would either lie about the total or collapse into a single root row.
